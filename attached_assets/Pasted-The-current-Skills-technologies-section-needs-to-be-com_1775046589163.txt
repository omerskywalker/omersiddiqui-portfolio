### STYLING RULES

- Cards use the existing dark card background (`background: rgba(255,255,255,0.03)` or the site's existing card bg)
- Left border: 3px solid secondary accent color (soft indigo `#818CF8`)
- Background numeral: font-size ~7rem, opacity 0.05, absolute positioned top-right of card, non-selectable
- Proof line: monospace font, font-size 0.75rem, color muted (slate-500 equivalent), margin-top auto (pin to bottom of card)
- Card title: font-weight 600, slightly larger than body
- Min card height: 220px on desktop so the numerals have room to breathe
- Animate cards in on scroll using IntersectionObserver: stagger 100ms between each card, fade-in + translateY(20px) → translateY(0)
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
